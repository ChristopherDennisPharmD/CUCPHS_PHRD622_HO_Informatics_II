# Transfer Scenario

## Encounter 001 Continued

The patient from Encounter 001 was admitted to a community hospital with suspected pneumonia, hypotension, hypoxemia, an elevated lactate, and worsening renal function. The patient received ceftriaxone, azithromycin, IV fluid, and supplemental oxygen.

The patient initially improved, but hypotension and respiratory distress subsequently worsened. The community hospital has arranged transfer to a tertiary-care ICU.

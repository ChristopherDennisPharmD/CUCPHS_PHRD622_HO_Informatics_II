# Complication Card 3

## Orders and Administrations Are Combined

The receiving organization receives the following medication list:

```text
Ceftriaxone 2 g IV
Azithromycin 500 mg IV
0.9% sodium chloride 1,000 mL IV
```

The list does not indicate whether each medication was ordered, dispensed, started, completed, held, or administered. It also omits the relevant times.

### Discuss

1. Why is the medication name and dose insufficient?
2. Which distinctions must the exchange preserve?
3. How could this ambiguity affect continued treatment?
4. Which information belongs in discrete fields, and which may require additional narrative context?

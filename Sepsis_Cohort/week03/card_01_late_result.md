# Complication Card 1

## A Result Arrives After the Summary

The sending clinician completed the transfer summary at 13:00. The patient departed at 13:15. At 13:25, the laboratory reported that the repeat lactate had increased to 4.2 mmol/L.

The completed transfer summary contains only the initial lactate of 3.6 mmol/L.

### Discuss

1. How should the receiving organization learn about the new result?
2. Is updating the original summary sufficient?
3. What should trigger the communication?
4. How should responsibility for reviewing the result be handled?

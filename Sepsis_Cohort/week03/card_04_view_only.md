# Complication Card 4

## Readable but Not Computable

The receiving clinician can open and read the transfer summary. The document includes allergies, medication administrations, laboratory results, and the reason for transfer.

The receiving EHR cannot place any of those data into its allergy list, medication history, laboratory flowsheet, or clinical decision-support tools. A clinician must enter the information manually if it should become part of the local structured record.

### Discuss

1. What can the clinician do with the information?
2. What can the receiving computer do with the information?
3. What risks accompany manual re-entry?
4. Does successful document delivery establish full interoperability?

# Week 3 Opening Slide Sequence

These slide-ready prompts introduce the activity before the Data Standards Part 2 lecture.

## Slide 1 Patient Transfer

**Title:** Transfer to a Tertiary-Care ICU

Encounter 001 has worsened despite antibiotics, oxygen, and IV fluids.

- Blood pressure has decreased to 84/48 mmHg.
- Oxygen requirement has increased to 4 L/min.
- A repeat lactate is pending.
- Transfer to a tertiary-care ICU has been accepted.

**Prompt:** What information does the receiving team need before the patient arrives?

## Slide 2 Information Across Systems

**Title:** The Clinical Record Is Distributed

| Information | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Patient and encounter information | Registration and transfer system |
| Vital signs and oxygen | Nursing flowsheet |
| Lactate and cultures | Laboratory and microbiology systems |
| Orders and administrations | Pharmacy system and medication record |
| Imaging interpretation | Radiology system |
| Clinical course | Physician documentation |

**Prompt:** How should these systems communicate with the receiving organization?

## Slide 3 Handoff Requirements

**Title:** Designing the Information Handoff

Consider:

- what must arrive before the patient;
- what may change during transport;
- what should populate discrete EHR fields;
- what needs narrative context; and
- what should remain together as a complete summary.

## Slide 4 Communication Complications

**Title:** When Information Exchange Falls Short

Reveal selected complication cards:

- A new result arrives after the transfer summary is complete.
- A value arrives without its unit or time.
- Medication orders and administrations are indistinguishable.
- A document is readable but cannot populate the EHR.
- Discrete data arrive without the clinical story.

**Prompt:** Was the information sent, understood, and made usable?

## Slide 5 Two Communication Needs

**Title:** Clinical Events and Clinical Summaries

| Individual events and updates | Complete clinical summary |
| --- | --- |
| Timely | Persistent |
| Discrete | Coherent |
| Computable | Human-readable |
| Can trigger workflow | Preserves context |

Introduce the terms **messaging standards** and **document standards** after students describe these needs.

## Slide 6 Lecture Transition

**Title:** Standards for Clinical Information Exchange

Healthcare systems need agreed-upon ways to:

- structure clinical information;
- transmit it to the intended recipient;
- preserve its meaning and source; and
- support appropriate review and action.

The lecture examines the standards and exchange approaches developed to meet these requirements.

# Complication Card 5

## Discrete Data Without the Clinical Story

The receiving system successfully imports the patient’s recent vital signs, laboratory results, medication administrations, and radiology report.

The receiving team does not receive the sending clinician’s reason for transfer, assessment of the likely infection source, description of the response to fluids, explanation of the conflicting allergy history, or list of pending follow-up needs.

### Discuss

1. What can the receiving team determine from the discrete data?
2. What remains uncertain without the clinical narrative?
3. Could every missing idea be represented adequately as an individual data field?
4. Why might the information need to remain together as a complete clinical summary?

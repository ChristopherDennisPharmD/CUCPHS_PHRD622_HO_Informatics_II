# Pharmacist Sepsis Review Design Template

Design one pharmacist-facing view for initial review after transfer. The space is limited: not every available item can receive equal prominence.

## Workflow decisions

1. What must the pharmacist see without searching?
2. What is best understood as a trend or timeline?
3. Which information can remain available behind a link or detail view?
4. Which conflicts or missing elements require a visible warning or assigned reconciliation task?
5. How will the pharmacist distinguish local information from external information?
6. How will the view show when each item was observed, resulted, received, and last updated?
7. How will it distinguish `not measured`, `not received`, `pending`, and `normal`?
8. What action should the pharmacist be able to take from the view?

## Sketch the view

Use the space below or a blank sheet to sketch the arrangement. Label:

- the patient-identity header;
- the highest-priority clinical information;
- trends or timelines;
- external-source indicators;
- warnings and reconciliation tasks; and
- the next pharmacist action.

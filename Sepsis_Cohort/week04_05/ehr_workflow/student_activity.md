# EHR Workflow Student Activity

## Designing the Pharmacist's Sepsis Review

Read `../shared_scenario.md`. Assume that appropriate access to the information has been approved. Your task is to make the information safe and useful within the pharmacist's workflow.

## Part 1 Match the patient

Review `patient_matching_case.md` and decide whether the imported information should be automatically matched, manually reviewed, or placed in a newly created record.

Document:

1. the fields that support the match;
2. the discrepancy that concerns you most;
3. the harm of attaching the information to the wrong patient;
4. the harm of failing to recognize duplicate records; and
5. the next verification step.

## Part 2 Evaluate the imported information

Review `information_inventory.md`. Select at least four items and discuss:

1. Can a person read the information?
2. Is its structure predictable?
3. Can the receiving system interpret and reuse its meaning?
4. Is the source visible?
5. Is the time or status clear?
6. What action, reconciliation, or follow-up does it require?

## Part 3 Design the view

Complete `design_template.md`. Your design should help a pharmacist answer:

- Is this the correct patient?
- Why was the patient transferred?
- What treatment has actually been administered?
- Is the patient improving or worsening?
- What results are pending or newly available?
- What information is uncertain or conflicting?
- What should I do next?

## Report out

In one minute, identify:

- one item that must be prominent;
- one item best displayed as a trend;
- one uncertainty that must not be hidden; and
- one action the interface should support.

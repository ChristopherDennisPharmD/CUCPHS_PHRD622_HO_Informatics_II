# Request 4: Predictive-Model Research Team

A university research team asks for the 100 encounters to develop a model that predicts clinical deterioration. The researchers request demographics, diagnoses, vital signs, laboratory results, medications, notes, ICU transfer, and mortality.

The team also asks to link the data with future encounters so model performance can be studied over time.

## Added detail

The initial request does not state whether the project has research review, patient authorization, a waiver, or a plan for data retention and destruction.

## Discuss

- Which questions must be answered before data are released?
- Could the purpose be met with fewer variables or reduced identifiers?
- What additional risk is introduced by longitudinal linkage?

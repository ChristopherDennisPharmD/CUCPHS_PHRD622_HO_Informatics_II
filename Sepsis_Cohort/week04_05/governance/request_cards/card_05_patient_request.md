# Request 5: Patient Access

After discharge, the patient requests an electronic copy of the complete record, including information received from the community hospital.

The portal displays local notes and results but does not display the outside transfer summary or the imported allergy entry. Staff suggest telling the patient to contact the sending hospital instead.

## Added detail

The patient wants the information before a follow-up visit and asks why records already visible to the care team are not visible in the portal.

## Discuss

- What should the health system verify before releasing information?
- How should source and uncertainty be communicated?
- What risk is created by unnecessary delay or technical limitation?

# Request 2: Sepsis Quality Team

The health system's sepsis quality team requests data from all 100 encounters. The team wants to calculate time to antibiotics, frequency of repeat lactate measurement, ICU transfer, and mortality.

The analysts initially request names, medical record numbers, complete notes, full medication records, and all laboratory results because it is easier to begin with the complete record.

## Added detail

The team expects to repeat the analysis every quarter and wants to retain a reusable dataset.

## Discuss

- Which elements are necessary for the stated measures?
- When might identifiers be needed, and when might a limited dataset be sufficient?
- What governance should apply to a reusable dataset?

# Request 3: External Technology Vendor

An external vendor supports the interface that imports laboratory results. The vendor requests remote access to production records to investigate why the repeat lactate did not appear promptly in the receiving EHR.

The vendor asks for continuous access for its entire support team so it can troubleshoot future problems without waiting for local staff.

## Added detail

The current services agreement does not clearly describe access to protected clinical information or the vendor's responsibilities if information is exposed.

## Discuss

- What information and level of access are needed to diagnose this problem?
- How could access be limited by person, purpose, system, or time?
- What agreements, monitoring, or technical safeguards may be appropriate?

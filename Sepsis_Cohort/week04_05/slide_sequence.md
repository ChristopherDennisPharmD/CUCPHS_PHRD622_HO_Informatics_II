# Weeks 4 and 5 Slide Sequence

These slide-ready prompts support a combined session. Slides 1-5 form the governance module; Slides 6-10 form the EHR workflow module. Either set can stand alone when the topics are taught in separate weeks.

## Slide 1 From Exchange to Use

**Title:** We Can Exchange the Information. What Comes Next?

The health system wants to:

- review sepsis care across 100 encounters; and
- build a pharmacist-facing sepsis review for transferred patients.

**Prompt:** Who should receive which information, for what purpose, and under what conditions?

## Slide 2 Information Requests

**Title:** Should This Sepsis Information Be Shared?

- Treating ICU pharmacist
- Sepsis quality team
- External technology vendor
- Predictive-model research team
- Patient requesting the record
- External health system providing subsequent treatment

**Prompt:** Would every requester need the same information and safeguards?

## Slide 3 Governance Questions

**Title:** Evaluate the Purpose, Scope, and Risk

Consider:

- identity and role;
- stated purpose;
- patients and data elements needed;
- need for direct identifiers;
- one-time or ongoing access;
- internal or external use;
- safeguards and oversight; and
- risks of granting and restricting access.

## Slide 4 Compare Recommendations

**Title:** More Than Yes or No

Possible recommendations:

- grant as requested;
- grant with limitations or safeguards;
- request more information; or
- decline or redirect.

**Prompt:** What condition most improves your recommendation?

## Slide 5 Governance Debrief

**Title:** Protect Information and Enable Appropriate Use

Good governance makes appropriate information available for a legitimate purpose while limiting unnecessary exposure and preserving accountability.

**Transition:** Appropriate access does not guarantee safe use inside the EHR.

## Slide 6 Which Patient?

**Title:** Match the Outside Information to a Local Record

Display the three candidates from `ehr_workflow/patient_matching_case.md`.

**Prompt:** Is this an automatic match, a manual review, or a new patient record?

## Slide 7 Information Arrived, but...

**Title:** What Can the Receiving Team Actually Use?

- A readable transfer summary is already outdated.
- A repeat lactate is discrete and newly available.
- A structured medication list obscures administration status.
- Allergy information is conflicting and unverified.
- A radiology report is available, but its image is not.
- A culture is still pending.

**Prompt:** Which problems involve transport, structure, meaning, or workflow?

## Slide 8 Design the Pharmacist View

**Title:** Not Every Item Can Be Prominent

Classify information as:

- prominent now;
- trend or timeline;
- available on demand;
- warning or reconciliation task; or
- excluded from this view.

## Slide 9 Make Uncertainty Visible

**Title:** Missing Does Not Mean Normal

Distinguish:

- not measured;
- not received;
- pending;
- unavailable;
- unverified or conflicting; and
- measured and within range.

Always preserve source and time.

## Slide 10 From EHR Design to CDS

**Title:** The Information Is Available and Usable

The pharmacist can now review the correct patient, understand the clinical course, identify uncertainty, and act.

**Next question:** Can the system identify which patients need review before the pharmacist opens the chart?

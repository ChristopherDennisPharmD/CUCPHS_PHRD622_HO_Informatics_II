# Week 1 Student Worksheet

## Reviewing a Fragmented Clinical Record

Your group has been asked to review one synthetic hospital encounter. You are not expected to diagnose the patient. Focus on what the available information allows you to determine and what makes the review difficult.

### Part 1 Patient review

1. What appears to be the primary reason for this hospitalization?

2. What changes in the patient's condition concern your group?

3. Is there evidence that infection may be present? Record the evidence and where you found it.

4. Is there evidence of new or worsening organ dysfunction? Record the evidence and where you found it.

5. What antimicrobial therapy, fluids, oxygen, or other supportive treatment has the patient received?

6. Construct a brief sequence of the most important events. Note any timestamps that are missing, inconsistent, or difficult to compare.

7. What additional information would you want before recommending an action?

### Part 2 Information experience

8. Which question was easiest to answer? Why?

9. Which question was most difficult to answer? Why?

10. Identify at least one example of information that was:

    - difficult to locate;
    - inconsistently described;
    - missing or uncertain; or
    - separated from other information needed to interpret it.

11. How could these information problems affect patient care?

12. What is one change to the information environment that would make the review easier or safer?

### Part 3 After the structured summary

13. Revise one of your initial answers using the structured summary.

14. What became easier to understand?

15. What important detail or context could still be lost in the structured summary?
